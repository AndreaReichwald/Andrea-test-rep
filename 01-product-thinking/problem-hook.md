# Problem Hook & Value Proposition, Module 1

- **Scenario:** My own initiative

## Strategic crisis
Staff workloads remain high, resulting in high costs for manual tasks. We will lose revenue due to instalment agreements that have not been finalised or have been cancelled.

## Moment of misery
Users either call or write us, some do not get in touch out of frustration, or simply because they forget. In such cases, we miss out on potential revenue. Still others agree to unrealistic instalment plans which they are then unable to keep to.

## Problem hook
Willing-to-pay customers abandon the instalment journey or call us because they can't tell what is affordable and safe for them, costing us recoveries, avoidable agent work and plans that don't hold.

## Value proposition
For willing-to-pay consumers like Mara, and the agents who pick up their unfinished cases,, we will offer a clear, explainable path to an affordable instalment plan in the portal, with a well-structured handoff to an agent only when needed, because too many consumers abandon or call when they can't tell what is affordable and safe, costing us recoveries, avoidable agent work and plans that don't hold, and Riverty's move to consistent digital self-service makes fixing this the next step..

## Cold-read self-review
Yes, recoveries are core to our business, and the instalment journey is where willing consumers are most likely to be lost, where agents spend avoidable effort, and where a poor plan destroys value, especially in purchased portfolios.
