Future-state journey: settling two claims in a few spare minutes

Persona: a shift worker with two open claims and a stable but variable income, who can't pay in full. Goal: settle both through a plan she can afford and keep, on her phone.

Confidence to proceed (illustrative, not measured)

1

Orient

User action

Opens link on phone→sees both claims and why each detail is needed.

Internal state

Wary and rushed→reassured before sharing anything sensitive.

Pain point addressed

No reason for requests→every ask explained in plain words.

2

Share

User action

Enters income as a range, adds documents later→never stuck, progress saved.

Internal state

Exposed and unsure→in control of what she shares and when.

Pain point addressed

Variable income, missing documents→no dead end, no call to Daniel.

3

Choose

User action

Adjusts one monthly amount across both claims→sees affordability and missed-payment consequences instantly.

Internal state

Cautious about consequences→confident the plan is safe to accept.

Pain point addressed

Affordability doubt, "could this make things worse?"→answered before she commits.

4

Keep

User action

Confirms once, then adjusts or pauses plan herself→keeps it when shifts change.

Internal state

Relieved and in control→trusts the plan and keeps paying.

Pain point addressed

Manual agent resolution→self-serve completion; agents handle exceptions only.

Replaces The manual loop: abandon at the income request, work it out alone, hunt for documents, call Daniel.

Three advantages over the manual workaround

  1. Minutes, not a queueShe finishes on her phone in spare minutes, instead of waiting on a call outside shift hours and repeating her details.
  2. Both claims, one planJoint handling is the default, instead of depending on an agent spotting the second claim.
  3. Guidance built inAffordability help, "why we ask" and missed-payment consequences replace the bank app, notes, inbox hunt and camera uploads.
Strategy: reclaim operational simplicity by stripping legacy noise. Value proposition: willing payers create and maintain affordable plans independently, improving recovery, lowering servicing cost and strengthening trust. Only the call to an agent is documented in the case study; the bank app, calculator, document hunt and camera uploads are assumptions to validate. The income range (stage 2) and plan pause (stage 4) need feasibility checks.
