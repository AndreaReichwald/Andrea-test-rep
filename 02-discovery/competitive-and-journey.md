- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** - The moment

The exact moment is Step 1: the income or document request, on her phone in a few spare minutes, followed by the failed FAQ detour. 

Core frustration: She is asked for something sensitive and effortful (income, documents) without knowing why, whether the plan is affordable, or what a missed payment would cost. When she looks for answers, the FAQ gives only basic information, she loses her place, and nothing she entered is saved.

- Why it feels broken
  - She is asked to hand over sensitive information before she has any reassurance
  - She can't tell whether accepting could make things worse
  - Her shift income varies, so "monthly income" has no single honest answer
  - If she can't find the document right now, there is no way forward in the journey
-The help is disconnected from the task. The FAQ pulls her out of the flow and doesn't answer her real questions.

- The result
  - She gives up and calls an agent
  - The journey fails at exactly the point where she needed guidance, not a form
